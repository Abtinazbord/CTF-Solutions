# CTF Writeups

Solutions and writeups for Capture The Flag challenges I have solved, currently covering Hack The Box across the forensics, cryptography, and miscellaneous categories.

The writeups are meant to show reasoning rather than just answers. Each one walks through what the challenge presented, what I noticed in the artifacts, the approaches I tried, and why the working one worked. Dead ends are included where they were instructive, since in forensics the process of ruling things out is most of the job.

## Structure

Each challenge lives in its own folder under its category:

- `README.md` covering the challenge and the full approach
- Any solution scripts written to solve it
- Supporting artifacts and notes from the investigation

## Categories

**Forensics** — log analysis, artifact recovery, and activity timeline reconstruction from provided evidence such as shell histories and service logs.

**Cryptography** — classical and modern cipher analysis, identifying the scheme in use and recovering plaintext.

**Miscellaneous** — challenges spanning encoding, scripting, and general problem solving.
